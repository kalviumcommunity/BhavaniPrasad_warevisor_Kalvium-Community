# CHURN REDUCTION INITIATIVE
## Executive Summary

### Situation
Customer churn is our leading revenue loss driver, costing $2M annually. Current churn rate is 7% vs industry average of 4%. We analyzed root causes across customer cohorts and identified support response latency as the single highest-impact retention bottleneck.

### Key Findings
- **Support speed directly impacts retention:** Customers receiving support within 2 hours churn at 3%. Customers waiting over 24 hours churn at 12%—a 4x difference.
- **Support latency is degrading:** Current support team averages a 6-hour response time. Response delays are our primary customer retention bottleneck.
- **High-value customers are most sensitive:** Accounts spending over $10K annually churn at 15% when experiencing support delays, driving disproportionate revenue loss.

### Business Risks
- **Revenue at Risk:** Maintaining the status quo 7% churn drains $2M annually. Losing high-value accounts compounds this financial exposure rapidly.
- **Competitive Vulnerability:** Competitors with faster support SLAs actively target dissatisfied accounts, turning service delays into market share loss.
- **Operational Degradation:** Ticket volume surged 40% year-over-year while response times degraded. Unaddressed overload will accelerate churn beyond 7%.

### Recommendations
1. **Hire 2 Support Engineers** (Cost: $200K/year)
   - Reduces average response time from 6 hours to under 2 hours.
   - Expected impact: Recovers $400K in annual retained revenue.
   - ROI: 2x return within first year.

2. **Implement Response Time SLA** (Cost: $0)
   - Establishes mandatory <2 hour target with automated daily performance tracking dashboard.
   - Drives operational accountability and priority routing.
   - Implementation target: Jan 1.

3. **Prioritize High-Value Customers** (Cost: $50K engineering)
   - Builds automated routing logic for accounts spending >$10K/year into a dedicated priority lane.
   - Expected impact: Reduces high-value segment churn by 50%.
   - Implementation timeline: Feb 1.

### Decision Needed
Approve hiring budget ($200K) and engineering allocation ($50K) by Dec 15. Total investment of $250K yields a projected $400K annual churn reduction in Year 1.
